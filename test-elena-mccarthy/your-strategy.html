Overview

Elena is a former Olympic swimmer coaching females online, doing about 3,000 EUR a month - almost all of it referrals, and referrals close at nearly 100%. The product converts when the right female gets in front of her. The problem was never the coaching, it's the pipeline. Her own words: "I seem to have a hard time finding clients that I want to work with and that want to work with me. How do I get to these people?"

The goal is 10,000 EUR a month. At her price of 4,000 EUR that's 2-3 closes a month once the machine runs. LinkedIn ads into a VSL funnel get strangers in front of her, and her delivery does the rest.

Three things make or break this build:

  • The offer is built for the avatar, not a Meta lead. New, easy, safe, big.
  • Speed to lead is the difference between success and failure. Dial inside 1-5 minutes.
  • Elena closes every call herself until she proves a 65%+ close rate. No setter.
  • Proof gets built as verified assets in the first 30 days - the testimonial videos already being filmed, the before and afters from the archive, and 5-10 LinkedIn recommendations. Nothing unverified goes on a public page.

Everything below flows from those four.

Sales System

Speed to lead

The single biggest lever. GHL fires instant lead alerts, the automated WhatsApp goes out inside 2 minutes, Elena dials inside 1-5 minutes. Conversion drops about 80% after 30 minutes.

The triage call (the qualifier)

A two-call close. Call one is a 10-minute diagnostic - qualify her, show personality, book the real call. No forms beyond name, email, phone. Referrals arrive warm; ad leads need this warm-up. It also means an unqualified lead costs you 10 minutes, not 60.

The main call and objection handling - referral closer, new to cold traffic

The one objection to drill: finances - your prospects' number one, from your own form. The reframes are scripted, not improvised:

  • The commitment reframe: "you were never financially committed to fixing this. That is why nothing stuck."
  • The standards analogy: Primark and Massimo Dutti both sell shirts, they are not the same purchase - and she already buys quality everywhere else in her life.
  • "I think I need someone in person" gets answered by the offer, not an argument: the live form audits mean you watch her move like an in-person coach would. Name it before she does.
  • Timing gets the honest mirror: there is no perfect week. The plan is built to survive imperfect ones.
  • Self-belief gets the business analogy: nobody builds anything hard alone - they bring in someone who's done it before and stays with them through it.

There are 13 ways to nudge someone over the line. Pick one or two per call. Always use their own words from the start of the call, gently, against them.
